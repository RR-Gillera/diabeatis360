the AI suggested, and the app says so under the photo ("Sample photo. Your meal may look different.").

`rice_plate.jpg` was also cropped to leave out the MyPlate logo in the corner. Licences that require it (CC BY, CC BY-SA)
are credited in the app under the photo, and in full here. CC BY-SA photos stay under the same licence after these
changes. No photo has a NonCommercial or NoDerivatives licence, because the app has a paid plan.
