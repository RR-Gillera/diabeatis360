## 1. Verdict: NO-GO for acceptance testing (for now)

There are no Critical defects, and most flows pass on the phone across all three roles. Acceptance testing should
still wait, for these reasons:

1. **Two graded modules weren't exercised at all this run.** Meal and exercise suggestions (module 4) and the
   nutrition scanner (module 5) were blocked: the Functions emulator has no Gemini key, so every call answered "busy".
   The run checked only the disclaimer, the quota display, the critical-reading pause, and that a failed call doesn't
   use up a credit.
2. **The main end-to-end chain never finished on one booking.** No video room opened on any surface. In part 1 the
   app called the live server (PAT-002). In part 2 there was no LiveKit set up, so nobody tried. A real two-phone
   call has still never been made (it was already open in October, D-03).
3. **One High defect is open: ADM-005.** Add Provider accepts a negative fee and auto-verifies the doctor.
   **Five Medium health-safety defects are open:**
   - DOC-009: a critical reading isn't marked red for the doctor;
   - DOC-010: the doctor's chart turns a 262 critical reading into a 174 daily average;
   - DOC-012: the rules accept a reading dated in the future;
   - ADM-009: the admin shows "Allergies: None" for a patient who never answered;
   - DOC-013: a child's account isn't marked as such for the doctor.
4. **The PAT-002 fix isn't committed.** `src/lib/server-target.ts` and its test are still untracked, and the edits
   to `ai.ts`, `video-room.ts` and `family-service.ts` are uncommitted.
5. **Nothing ran on the release APK**, and no doctor flow ran on a phone.

**What GO needs:**
- Fix items 1–9 of section 9.
- Re-run the blocked items with a local Gemini key and LiveKit (section 8).
- One pass of the main chain on the release APK on two phones.

**What works well, and is worth showing the panel:**
- Booking, accept and decline arrive in real time.
- Past slots are refused, and taken slots are greyed out.
- All 22 D4 boundary values classify correctly, impossible readings are refused, and the D44 edit history works.
- A critical reading shows Call 911, alerts the doctor and pauses AI.
- Firestore privacy holds: a doctor can read nothing before a booking, and only the booked patient after it.
- The consultation lifecycle works: end, notes, read-only chat, summary.
- Times stay in Philippine time on phones set to New York or London.
- Revenue and earnings totals match a hand calculation exactly (D3 as amended by D37).
- The guardian flow works: pending, booking blocked, approved, booking allowed.
- Deactivated accounts are refused, and announcements reach the right audience.
