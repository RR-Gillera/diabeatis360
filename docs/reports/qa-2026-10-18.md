## 1. Verdict

- **Ran:** 197 checks: **156 PASS, 32 FAIL, 9 NEEDS HUMAN**. There are also 49 findings: 6 High, 19 Medium, 24 Low, including 2 change requests from the team.
- **Works well:**
  - booking, accept and decline in real time across two phones;
  - chat;
  - ending a consultation and the notes with advice and follow-up;
  - AI limits, the allergy filter and the critical-reading pause;
  - the scanner allergy warning;
  - Firestore privacy (nobody saw another person's data);
  - every admin action tested.
- **Top risks:**
  1. **Health profile data loss.** Saving Profile on a new phone wipes the stored birthdate, activity and diet.
  2. **Allergies can be skipped.** Leaving onboarding halfway marks it finished, so allergies are never asked.
  3. **Video calls don't connect.** Public meet.jit.si asks for a moderator login.
  4. **The installed app asks for the password every time it opens.**
  5. **Past time slots can be booked.**
  6. **Mock payment isn't labelled as a demo** on the consultation checkout.
  7. **Medical-safety wording.** "calculate dosages"; no one-tap emergency call on a critical reading.
- **Ready for acceptance testing? Not yet.** Fix the Critical and High items in section 8 first (about 1–2 days of work), then repeat the affected checks on a phone with the APK.

## 5. UX findings and suggested improvements

1. **Change request UX-CR1 (team):** show Android's own "Allow notifications?" prompt instead of the 5-switch panel; per-type choices can live in Profile.
2. **Change request UX-CR2 (team):** Messenger-style call entries in the chat:
   - "Missed video call · Call again";
   - "Call declined";
   - "Video call · 5 min 32 s".

   Today calls leave nothing in the chat; the Calls document already has the status and times.
3. **First-time patient:** Register → Home is 10 screens and 29 taps/fields. Suggestions:
   - show the password rule up front;
   - fix the progress bar (it goes back from 25% to 20%);
   - resume onboarding where the person left (D-02).
4. **Safety wording:** remove "dosages" (D-08); add a one-tap emergency call on critical readings (D-07); keep the existing disclaimers. They are on every AI screen and in the chat, which is good.
5. **Feedback:**
   - an offline message instead of an endless "Saving…" (D-17);
   - a reason when Save is greyed out;
   - "Delete reading?" confirmation (D-13);
   - "End without notes?" prompt.
6. **Honesty:**
   - demo labels on the consultation payment (D-06);
   - remove the switches for unbuilt features (D-21);
   - "Premium" is not "unlimited";
   - the Filipino option either works or is hidden (D-18).
7. **Errors:** replace the raw rules text with "This appointment isn't available" (D-16).
8. **Child accounts:** tell a child account with guardian approval pending that it can't book *before* it picks a time.
